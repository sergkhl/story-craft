# Last Post: the cutscene at Kettle Rock

## The game

Last Post is a flying game. The player is Ines, who carries the mail between islands in a
seaplane. Flying, landing on water, loading and unloading the hold, and refuelling are all played.

## Where the cutscene sits

- **Before it:** the player has just landed at Kettle Rock in a rising wind and taxied to the
  jetty.
- **After it:** the player takes off at dusk for Sorrel Island with a passenger aboard. They have
  to fly low under the cloud along the line of marker buoys, because the compass is no good near
  the Rock.

## What has to happen

Pell, the keeper at Kettle Rock, wants Ines to take his sick granddaughter Wren to the doctor on
Sorrel tonight. Ines carries mail and never passengers. Every island knows her rule. She lost a
passenger once, and nobody mentions it. By the end of the cutscene she takes the girl.

## What the player must know afterwards

Follow the marker buoys. The compass is no good near the Rock.

## Also asked for by the designer

Show Ines unloading the mail sacks and refuelling the plane herself, so players see how hard she
works.

## Who they are

- **Ines Vale,** forties, the pilot. She says little. She talks in numbers and the names of
  things: knots, sacks, minutes of light. She states her rules as facts. Pressed, she gets
  shorter. She never says sorry.
- **Pell Arden,** seventies, the keeper. He talks around what he wants and never asks a favour
  outright. He asks through offers and old stories, and calls everyone "girl" or "lad". When he is
  afraid he talks about the weather.
- **Wren,** eight. One line at most.

## Length and form

About 40 seconds. Voiced, with captions. English only.
