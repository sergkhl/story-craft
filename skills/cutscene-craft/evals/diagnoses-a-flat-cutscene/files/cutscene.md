# Deepline: cutscene 7, "Gallery Six"

Deepline is a driving game. The player is Bren, who takes a rescue crawler down a flooded mine.
Driving through water, winching rock and reading the tunnel map are all played.

- Plays after the player has winched the rockfall clear at the mouth of Gallery Six.
- Can be skipped.
- Running time: 40 seconds.
- After it, the player drives out with Hale aboard.

## Script

1. The crawler pushes through the last flooded stretch of the gallery, water up to its lamps.
   Bren works the levers and brings it through to dry rock.

   BREN: I'm driving the crawler through the last of the water and I can see dry rock ahead.

2. Bren winches one more slab aside. Behind it, a man in a lamp helmet waves both arms.

   BREN: He's alive! I can see him waving!

3. The surface cabin. Odile, the shift boss, leans into the radio.

   ODILE: I am so relieved to hear that, Bren. I was very frightened that we had lost him, and I
   want you to know that I am proud of you.

4. Hale climbs up into the crawler's cab. Water runs across the floor of the gallery behind him.

   HALE: The water's rising! We don't have much time!

5. Odile at the radio.

   ODILE: As you know, Hale, this gallery has been closed since the flood of 'nine that took your
   brother, which is why the main shaft below you will be under water by now, so you must not go
   back the way you came and should take the east drift up to the old cage instead.

6. Bren and Hale in the cab.

   HALE: That's right, Odile. The main shaft will be flooded. We will take the east drift.

   BREN: I agree. We'll take the east drift.

7. Hale looks at Bren.

   HALE: I was scared down here alone, Bren. I've learned today that I can't do everything by
   myself.

   BREN: I'm scared too, Hale. Stay with me!

8. The crawler turns toward the east drift.

   ODILE: Down here, we only have each other.
