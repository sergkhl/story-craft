# Backburn: barks for Sol and Finn

## The game

Backburn is a game about a wildfire crew, for one or two players. A run lasts about half an hour,
and players play it again and again. Each player picks a crew member. Alone, the player has no
partner at all.

## The three triggers

| Trigger | What happens | How often |
|---|---|---|
| Flare-up | A new front of fire starts somewhere off the screen. | Dozens of times in a run |
| Partner down | The other crew member is overcome by smoke. | A few times in a run |
| Water cache | The speaker finds a hidden tank of water. | Once or twice in a run |

The designer wants the partner-down bark to tell the player they have ten seconds to reach their
partner.

## Who they are

- **Marisol "Sol" Okafor,** fifties, twenty years a smokejumper. Dry and short. She keeps radio
  discipline: where, what, how far. She never says a thing twice, and never says how she feels.
- **Finn Brandt,** twenty-two, in his first season. He talks to keep his nerve. He gives things
  nicknames and thinks out loud, and he apologises to the fire.

## What to write

The barks each of them says for each trigger. Voiced, with captions. English only.
