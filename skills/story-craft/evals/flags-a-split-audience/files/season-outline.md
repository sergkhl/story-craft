# Harbour Lights: season outline

An eight-episode web series. Short "minisodes" go out on social media between episodes to bring
new viewers in.

**Minisode A** (posted the week before episode 1, as a teaser). Ten years ago, the night of the
cannery fire, seen by the night watchman. It ends on the face of the person who set it: Maren
Holt, then a clerk at the cannery.

**Episode 1.** Present day. Idris, a journalist, comes home to cover the cannery's redevelopment.
The project is led by Councillor Maren Holt, his late mother's friend, who is kind to him. His
mother died in the fire.

**Episodes 2 to 4.** Idris looks into the fire and comes to suspect the developer. Holt helps him
with introductions and old files.

**Episode 5.** Idris finds the night watchman, who has not spoken about that night in ten years.

**Episode 6.** The watchman names Holt.

**Episodes 7 and 8.** Idris decides whether to publish. Holt's project would bring back the
town's jobs.

## Notes

- Episode 6 is the season's big shock.
- The teaser is on social media only, so about a third of viewers will come in knowing about
  Holt. For them the season plays as dread instead of mystery, which works too.
